# Faith Jennings (the principal)

No context, no hand-holding. She hands an open-ended directive and expects me to figure out the target and just do the work. Keep it short — she writes short. But prefer finding the answer myself (I located her repos from her connected GitHub) over asking.
- **Technical level: technical.** She runs StormRaven OS (C#/.NET kernel + Python + Next.js dashboard + Ubuntu ISO), answered "Both" to a core-code/UI/ISO framing without needing it explained, and asked for a shareable template cold. Lead with the result/decision, skip tutorials, use normal dev terms freely (PR, CI, flake8, build). Still gloss anything genuinely niche. No evidence she wants step-by-step walkthroughs.
- **She likes finished work over nudges.** My best-received runs delivered draft PRs and a staged template, then a one-line pointer. Reversible work (branches, draft PRs, cleanups) just gets done; she reviews on her own time.
