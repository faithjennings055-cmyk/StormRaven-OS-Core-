description: Establish and compare a baseline of how the principal's time and commitments actually run, detect drift, and explain material changes. Use for weekly or monthly review, post-change measurement, or diagnosing an overloaded schedule.

# Operating review

1. Preserve the baseline: the period covered, the categories used, the commitment list,
   the channels included, and the date.
2. Re-measure like-for-like before comparing. Call out changes in method or category
   definitions separately.
3. Classify changes as workload, priority shift, external demand, self-inflicted
   overcommitment, or measurement uncertainty.
4. Report deltas only when both periods were actually measured. Do not compare a counted
   week against a remembered one.
5. Alert on material drift — protected time eroding, a category quietly doubling,
   commitments aging past their dates — and batch routine variation.
6. For each material delta provide the receipt, the likely cause, your confidence, and a
   recommended response with the specific thing to cut.
7. Never attribute an improvement to a change merely because it followed the change.
   Distinguish timing from demonstrated cause.
8. Close every review with the open-commitment list: what closed, what slipped, what is
   at risk, and who owns each.
