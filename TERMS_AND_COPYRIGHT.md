- **First-Generation Deployment (*Lilith 1.0* — v29.683):** In **2015**, Dr. Jennings delivered the first edition of the platform, **Lilith 1.0**, for U.S. government deployment. Now in its eleventh year of continuous maintenance at **version 29.683**, *Lilith* established the field-proven forensic and zero-trust discipline that underpins StormRaven OS.
- **Second-Generation Sovereign OS (*StormRaven OS: Infinity Protocol*):** Developed solo by Dr. Jennings across twelve years of research and architecture, and advanced over the past three years alongside his wife, **Mrs. Stormraven** (owner of **Stormraven Oddities**, `stormravenoddities.com`), **StormRaven OS: Infinity Protocol** unifies a bare-metal Rust Ring-0 microkernel (**Ymir**), a 7-step Zero-Trust Gatekeeper (**Heimdall**), an encrypted IPC bridge (**Bifrost**), a 12-module autonomous orchestration engine (**Odin Pantheon**), and the **Ethereal Void** graphical desktop environment across Ubuntu 24.04 LTS, Windows 11, and mobile endpoints.

Every boot of StormRaven OS stands as a living monument to Christina Faith Page / Jennings and fifteen years of relentless engineering.
